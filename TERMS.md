# Terms of Use

**Application:** FinArchive
**Last updated:** 2026-09-21

## What this application is

FinArchive is a private, personal application written by its author for their
own use. It reads the author's own bank accounts and keeps a record of them.

It is **not** a product, a service, or an offer of one. It has no users other
than its author, no sign-up, no accounts to create, no support, and nothing to
purchase. Nothing here constitutes an invitation to use it.

## Who may use it

Its author, on their own accounts. Nobody else is granted any right to use it,
and there is no mechanism by which anybody else could.

## What it does

It requests read-only access to payment accounts that its author has personally
linked and authorised, retrieves account information and transaction records,
and stores them in a database its author controls.

It does not initiate payments, transfer money, place orders, or make any change
at any institution.

## Access, and ending it

Access to each institution exists only while the author's consent stands. That
consent is granted at the institution, expires automatically, and can be
withdrawn at any time. Withdrawal ends the application's access immediately.

The author may stop using the application, revoke every consent, and delete
everything it holds, at any time and for any reason.

The application is used by its author at their own risk and is provided with no
warranty of any kind, express or implied. It may be wrong, incomplete, or
unavailable. It is a record-keeping aid and not a source of truth: the
institutions themselves remain authoritative for what happened to the money, and
any figure that matters should be checked against them.

## No liability to third parties

Because the application serves nobody but its author, and processes nobody
else's data, no duty is owed to any third party and no liability to one can
arise from its use.
